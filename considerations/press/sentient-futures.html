Jørn Lager Lyngås joins Sentient Futures as AI research fellow

Jørn Lager Lyngås, founder and managing partner of VAERD & Co., has joined the AI×Animals fellowship at San Francisco based Sentient Futures.

The fellowship runs through the autumn of 2026. Fellows from animal welfare, the AI industry, governance and academia work through precision livestock farming, interspecies communication, genetic welfare, AI in alternative proteins and the long-term questions AI raises for all sentient beings.

Lyngås is a social anthropologist from the University of Oslo. In the fellowship he looks at how algorithmic standardisation affects individuality, in animal welfare and in organisations, and at what happens to tacit knowledge passed down through generations of practice when more of the work moves from people to AI agents.

"Companies meet these as AI questions, but they are about people and the knowledge they carry. The fellowship lets me work on them together with people from the AI industry, animal welfare and policy," says Lyngås.

The work feeds directly into VAERD's advice on AI, and his notes from the fellowship will be published under Considerations.
